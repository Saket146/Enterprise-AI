# Enterprise Data Dictionary

## Core Business Domains

### 1. Organization

Purpose:
Stores the organizational hierarchy.

Entities:

- Departments
- Employees
- Roles
- Permissions

---

### 2. Project Management

Purpose:
Tracks all enterprise projects.

Entities:

- Projects
- Tasks
- Milestones
- Teams

---

### 3. Human Resources

Purpose:
Employee lifecycle.

Entities:

- Leave Requests
- Payroll
- Performance Reviews
- Attendance

---

### 4. Finance

Purpose:
Financial operations.

Entities:

- Budgets
- Expenses
- Invoices
- Vendors

---

### 5. Sales

Purpose:
Customer management.

Entities:

- Clients
- Opportunities
- Contracts

---

### 6. Knowledge Management

Purpose:
Enterprise knowledge.

Entities:

- Documents
- Policies
- Reports
- Meeting Notes
- Emails

---

### 7. AI Operations

Purpose:
Data generated by the AI Operating System.

Entities:

- Agent Logs
- Chat Sessions
- Workflow Executions
- Audit Logs
